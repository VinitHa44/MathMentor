# Practice Problems: Parabola and Ellipse (20 Problems)

## PARABOLA PROBLEMS

## Problem 1: Basic Parabola
**Question:** Find focus and directrix of y² = 16x

**Solution:**
Compare with y² = 4ax
4a = 16 → a = 4

Focus: (a, 0) = (4, 0)
Directrix: x = -a = -4

**Answer:** Focus (4, 0), Directrix x = -4

---

## Problem 2: Vertex Form
**Question:** Find equation of parabola with vertex (0, 0), axis along x-axis, passing through (4, 4)

**Solution:**
Equation: y² = 4ax

Substitute (4, 4):
16 = 4a(4)
a = 1

Equation: y² = 4x

**Answer:** y² = 4x

---

## Problem 3: Latus Rectum
**Question:** Find length of latus rectum of y² = 12x

**Solution:**
y² = 4ax
4a = 12 → a = 3

Length of latus rectum = 4a = 12

**Answer:** 12

---

## Problem 4: Tangent to Parabola
**Question:** Find equation of tangent to y² = 8x at point (2, 4)

**Solution:**
yy₁ = 2a(x + x₁)
y(4) = 2(2)(x + 2)
4y = 4(x + 2)
y = x + 2

**Answer:** y = x + 2

---

## Problem 5: Focal Chord
**Question:** If focal chord of y² = 16x has one end at (4, 8), find other end

**Solution:**
For focal chord, t₁t₂ = -1

At (4, 8): 4 = 4t₁², 8 = 8t₁
t₁ = 1

t₂ = -1

Other end: (4(-1)², 8(-1)) = (4, -8)

**Answer:** (4, -8)

---

## Problem 6: Parametric Point
**Question:** Find point on y² = 12x at parameter t = 3

**Solution:**
x = at² = 3(9) = 27
y = 2at = 2(3)(3) = 18

**Answer:** (27, 18)

---

## Problem 7: Normal Equation
**Question:** Find equation of normal to y² = 8x at (2, 4)

**Solution:**
Slope of tangent: dy/dx at (2, 4)
From y² = 8x: 2y·dy/dx = 8
dy/dx = 4/y = 4/4 = 1

Slope of normal = -1

Normal: y - 4 = -1(x - 2)
y = -x + 6

**Answer:** x + y = 6

---

## Problem 8: Tangent with Slope
**Question:** Find equation of tangent to y² = 16x with slope 2

**Solution:**
y = mx + a/m
y = 2x + 4/2
y = 2x + 2

**Answer:** y = 2x + 2

---

## Problem 9: Chord of Contact
**Question:** Find chord of contact from (4, 4) to y² = 8x

**Solution:**
yy₁ = 2a(x + x₁)
y(4) = 2(2)(x + 4)
4y = 4(x + 4)
y = x + 4

**Answer:** y = x + 4

## Problem 10: Position of Point
**Question:** Check if (1, 5) lies inside, on, or outside y² = 16x

**Solution:**

## ELLIPSE PROBLEMS

## Problem 11: Basic Ellipse
**Question:** Find foci of x²/25 + y²/16 = 1

**Solution:**
a² = 25, b² = 16
a = 5, b = 4

e = √(1 - b²/a²) = √(1 - 16/25) = √(9/25) = 3/5

Foci: (±ae, 0) = (±3, 0)

**Answer:** (3, 0) and (-3, 0)

---

## Problem 12: Eccentricity
**Question:** Find eccentricity of ellipse with major axis 10 and minor axis 8

**Solution:**
2a = 10 → a = 5
2b = 8 → b = 4

e = √(1 - b²/a²) = √(1 - 16/25) = 3/5

**Answer:** 3/5

---

## Problem 13: Vertices
**Question:** Find vertices of x²/36 + y²/20 = 1

**Solution:**
a² = 36, b² = 20
a = 6 (major axis along x)

Vertices: (±6, 0)

**Answer:** (6, 0) and (-6, 0)

---

## Problem 14: Latus Rectum of Ellipse
**Question:** Find length of latus rectum of x²/25 + y²/9 = 1

**Solution:**
a² = 25, b² = 9
a = 5, b = 3

Latus rectum = 2b²/a = 2(9)/5 = 18/5

**Answer:** 18/5

---

## Problem 15: Tangent to Ellipse
**Question:** Find equation of tangent to x²/16 + y²/9 = 1 at (2, 3√3/2)

**Solution:**
xx₁/a² + yy₁/b² = 1
x(2)/16 + y(3√3/2)/9 = 1
x/8 + y√3/6 = 1

Multiply by 24:
3x + 4y√3 = 24

**Answer:** 3x + 4y√3 = 24

---

## Problem 16: Parametric Form
**Question:** Find point on x²/25 + y²/16 = 1 at θ = π/3

**Solution:**
x = a·cos(θ) = 5·cos(π/3) = 5/2
y = b·sin(θ) = 4·sin(π/3) = 4√3/2 = 2√3

**Answer:** (5/2, 2√3)

---

## Problem 17: Director Circle
**Question:** Find equation of director circle of x²/25 + y²/16 = 1

**Solution:**
Director circle: x² + y² = a² + b²
= 25 + 16 = 41

**Answer:** x² + y² = 41

---

## Problem 18: Focal Property
**Question:** For ellipse x²/25 + y²/9 = 1, verify focal property at point (5, 0)

**Solution:**
Foci: (±4, 0)

PF₁ = |5 - 4| = 1
PF₂ = |5 - (-4)| = 9

PF₁ + PF₂ = 1 + 9 = 10 = 2a ✓

**Answer:** Verified (sum = 10 = 2a)

---

## Problem 19: Auxiliary Circle
**Question:** Find equation of auxiliary circle of x²/16 + y²/9 = 1

**Solution:**
Auxiliary circle has radius = a

x² + y² = a² = 16

**Answer:** x² + y² = 16

---

## Problem 20: Area of Ellipse
**Question:** Find area of ellipse x²/9 + y²/4 = 1

**Solution:**
a² = 9 → a = 3
b² = 4 → b = 2

Area = πab = π(3)(2) = 6π

**Answer:** 6π
