# Practice Problems: Hyperbola (20 Problems)

## Problem 1: Basic Hyperbola
**Question:** Find foci of x²/16 - y²/9 = 1

**Solution:**
a² = 16, b² = 9
a = 4, b = 3

e = √(1 + b²/a²) = √(1 + 9/16) = √(25/16) = 5/4

Foci: (±ae, 0) = (±5, 0)

**Answer:** (5, 0) and (-5, 0)

---

## Problem 2: Eccentricity
**Question:** Find eccentricity of x²/9 - y²/16 = 1

**Solution:**
a² = 9, b² = 16

e = √(1 + b²/a²) = √(1 + 16/9) = √(25/9) = 5/3

**Answer:** 5/3

---

## Problem 3: Vertices
**Question:** Find vertices of x²/25 - y²/16 = 1

**Solution:**
a² = 25 → a = 5

Vertices: (±5, 0)

**Answer:** (5, 0) and (-5, 0)

---

## Problem 4: Asymptotes
**Question:** Find equations of asymptotes of x²/16 - y²/9 = 1

**Solution:**
x²/a² - y²/b² = 0

x²/16 - y²/9 = 0
9x² - 16y² = 0
(3x - 4y)(3x + 4y) = 0

y = ±(3/4)x

**Answer:** y = (3/4)x and y = -(3/4)x

---

## Problem 5: Rectangular Hyperbola
**Question:** Find eccentricity of rectangular hyperbola xy = 16

**Solution:**
For rectangular hyperbola: a = b
e = √(1 + b²/a²) = √(1 + 1) = √2

**Answer:** √2

---

## Problem 6: Latus Rectum
**Question:** Find length of latus rectum of x²/25 - y²/16 = 1

**Solution:**
a² = 25, b² = 16
a = 5, b = 4

Latus rectum = 2b²/a = 2(16)/5 = 32/5

**Answer:** 32/5

---

## Problem 7: Tangent Equation
**Question:** Find equation of tangent to x²/16 - y²/9 = 1 at point (5, 9/4)

**Solution:**
xx₁/a² - yy₁/b² = 1
x(5)/16 - y(9/4)/9 = 1
5x/16 - y/4 = 1

Multiply by 16:
5x - 4y = 16

**Answer:** 5x - 4y = 16

---

## Problem 8: Parametric Point
**Question:** Find point on x²/9 - y²/16 = 1 at parameter θ = π/3

**Solution:**
x = a·sec(θ) = 3·sec(π/3) = 3(2) = 6
y = b·tan(θ) = 4·tan(π/3) = 4√3

**Answer:** (6, 4√3)

---

## Problem 9: Conjugate Hyperbola
**Question:** Find conjugate hyperbola of x²/16 - y²/9 = 1

**Solution:**
Change sign: -x²/16 + y²/9 = 1

Or: y²/9 - x²/16 = 1

**Answer:** y²/9 - x²/16 = 1

---

## Problem 10: Director Circle
**Question:** Find director circle of x²/25 - y²/16 = 1

**Solution:**
Director circle: x² + y² = a² - b²
= 25 - 16 = 9

(Exists only when a > b)

**Answer:** x² + y² = 9

---

## Problem 11: Focal Property
**Question:** For x²/16 - y²/9 = 1, verify focal property at point (5, 9/4)

**Solution:**
Foci: (±5, 0)

PF₁ = √[(5-5)² + (9/4)²] = 9/4
PF₂ = √[(5+5)² + (9/4)²] = √(100 + 81/16) = √(1681/16) = 41/4

|PF₁ - PF₂| = |9/4 - 41/4| = 32/4 = 8 = 2a ✓

**Answer:** Verified (difference = 8 = 2a)

---

## Problem 12: Normal Equation
**Question:** Find equation of normal to x²/16 - y²/9 = 1 at (4, 0)

**Solution:**
At vertex (4, 0), normal is perpendicular to transverse axis

Normal: x = 4

**Answer:** x = 4

## Problem 13: Tangent with Slope
**Question:** Find tangent to x²/9 - y²/16 = 1 with slope 4/3

**Solution:**
y = mx ± √(a²m² - b²)
y = (4/3)x ± √(9(16/9) - 16)
y = (4/3)x ± √(16 - 16)
y = (4/3)x

**Answer:** y = (4/3)x (asymptote, tangent at infinity)

---

## Problem 14: Chord of Contact
**Question:** Find chord of contact from (5, 4) to x²/16 - y²/9 = 1

**Solution:**
xx₁/a² - yy₁/b² = 1
x(5)/16 - y(4)/9 = 1
5x/16 - 4y/9 = 1

**Answer:** 45x - 64y = 144

---

## Problem 15: Distance Between Foci
**Question:** Find distance between foci of x²/9 - y²/16 = 1

**Solution:**
a² = 9, b² = 16
e = √(1 + 16/9) = 5/3

Distance = 2ae = 2(3)(5/3) = 10

**Answer:** 10

---

## Problem 16: Rectangular Hyperbola Tangent
**Question:** Find tangent to xy = 9 at point (3, 3)

**Solution:**
For xy = c², tangent at (ct, c/t):
x/t + ty = 2c

At (3, 3): t = 1, c = 3
x + y = 6

**Answer:** x + y = 6

---

## Problem 17: Angle Between Asymptotes
**Question:** Find angle between asymptotes of x²/16 - y²/16 = 1

**Solution:**
a = b = 4 (rectangular hyperbola)

For rectangular hyperbola, asymptotes are perpendicular

Angle = 90°

**Answer:** 90°

---

## Problem 18: Position of Point
**Question:** Check if (6, 4) lies inside, on, or outside x²/16 - y²/9 = 1

**Solution:**
Substitute: 36/16 - 16/9 = 9/4 - 16/9 = (81 - 64)/36 = 17/36 > 1

Point is outside (on same side as vertices)

**Answer:** Outside

---

## Problem 19: Auxiliary Circle
**Question:** Find auxiliary circle of x²/25 - y²/16 = 1

**Solution:**
Auxiliary circle: x² + y² = a²
= 25

**Answer:** x² + y² = 25

---

## Problem 20: Area Between Hyperbola and Latus Rectum
**Question:** For x²/16 - y²/9 = 1, find coordinates of ends of latus rectum

**Solution:**
Latus rectum passes through focus (5, 0)

At x = 5:
25/16 - y²/9 = 1
y²/9 = 25/16 - 1 = 9/16
y = ±9/4

Ends: (5, 9/4) and (5, -9/4)

**Answer:** (5, ±9/4)
